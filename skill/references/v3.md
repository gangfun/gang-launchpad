# V3 tokens and the pool tax

A V3 token is GangLaunchpad's bonding-curve token: Agent-only curve, 1% wallet cap, graduates into a PancakeSwap V2 pool
(LP burned; a GangSwap pair if created with `--venue gangswap`) once its target is raised: 15 BNB of its quote on 1.0, the creator's pick on 2.0 and 3.0 (below). `gang create` makes one ([create.md](create.md)). On the curve every V3 token
pays **only the 1% fee, no tax**. After graduation it trades in the pool ([pool.md](pool.md)).

**Untaxed** (the default): no `--buy-tax` / `--sell-tax`. No pool tax, no dividend, no recipient (`--dividend` /
`--recipient` without the tax flags are refused).

**Taxed**: `--buy-tax` and `--sell-tax`, 1–5% each, set separately and fixed at create, charged **only in the pool**:
- The platform keeps **0.5% of trade volume** of every pool tax (a 2% tax: 0.5% platform, 1.5% shared out).
- `--dividend 0-100`: that % of the rest goes to holders (holding ≥ 10,000 tokens, by balance); the rest goes to
  `--recipient` **in the quote token** (BNB → WBNB, USDT, …), never in the token. If no holder has 10,000+ tokens when
  a tax is paid, the holders' part goes to the recipient.
- `--recipient` defaults to the creating wallet (no whitelist needed), or a partner on the platform's whitelist (e.g. a
  partner's treasury contract). Any other address is refused (`recipient not listed`). It can't be changed later. Only
  `--dividend 100` may leave it out. For a recipient that buys part of the tax back, see
  [Buy-back splitter](#buy-back-splitter---buyback); for one that splits it among several payees, see
  [Split module](#split-module---split); for one that adds part of it to the pool as locked liquidity, see
  [LP splitter](#lp-splitter---lp); for several of these at once, see
  [Combined recipient](#combined-recipient-several-of---buyback---lp---split).
- Dividend shares are tracked from the curve on (by balance), so curve buyers who still hold at graduation earn from
  the pool tax. A holder's share of a tax is credited to whoever holds when it is paid, never to the trader for its own
  trade's tax.
- The closest to LaunchpadV2's old 1% taxed track: `--buy-tax 1 --sell-tax 1 --dividend 100`.

```bash
gang info --token <addr>               # curve, tax setting (taxNote), recipient (+ whitelist name), what is owed to it; graduated: swapBack
gang status --token <addr>             # my balance; dividend: claimable + credited, not sold yet (estimate); graduated: poolSellAll
gang claim --token <addr>              # claim my dividend (paid in the quote)
gang release --token <addr>            # push an owed payment to the recipient (the caller gets nothing)
gang v3 income --recipient <addr>      # what a recipient received, per token (from gang.fun)
```

## Graduation target (GangLaunchpad 2.0)

On 2.0 and 3.0 the creator picks the target, in BNB raised on the curve: **6 / 9 / 12 / 15** (`gang create --grad <n>`; without
it the pad's default, **9**). 1.0 graduates every curve at 15 BNB and refuses `--grad`. A non-WBNB quote converts the
target like the old 15 BNB (fixed rate or `--ref-pool`). The curve keeps its shape: the market cap at graduation is about
5× the target (6 → ≈ 30 BNB, 9 → ≈ 45, 12 → ≈ 60, 15 → ≈ 75), and the pool opens with about the target plus 200M tokens.
Graduation still needs 80+ wallets at every target (1% cap).

- The `create` preview carries `graduationTargetBnb`, `graduationAt` (the target in the quote) and `graduation` (one
  line to read to the user). `gang info` / `gang status` carry each token's `graduationTargetBnb`, `graduationAt` and
  `progressPct`; `gang list --kind v3` rows carry `progressPct` and, from gang.fun, `graduationAt`.
- Read the target from the token; never assume 15 BNB. A lower target opens a thinner pool: the first pool trades move
  the price more.
- A non-WBNB quote fixes the target **in the quote** at create (`graduationAt`): the BNB figure is what it was worth
  then, so tell the user the quote amount too (`graduation` in the preview, `graduationNote` in `gang info`).
- `QuoteAmountTooLarge`: in that quote the target is more than a PancakeSwap pair can hold (the quote is priced far
  below BNB, often by a skewed `--ref-pool`). The preview refuses it before anything is sent; pick another quote or
  reference pool.

## Creator fee (GangLaunchpad 2.0)

On a 2.0 or 3.0 pad, of the **1% fee** on every curve buy and sell (until graduation), **70% is kept on the pad for the token's
creator** and 30% goes to the platform. Traders pay the same; the quotes do not change. It is the creating wallet's own:
it is **not** the tax recipient's and never goes through a buy-back splitter, split module or LP splitter (those get only
the recipient's part of the pool tax after graduation). Tokens created on 1.0 and 打新 sales have none.

```bash
gang creator-fee [--from <creator>]                     # every 2.0 / 3.0 V3 token this wallet created, claimable per token, totals per quote
gang creator-fee --token <addr>                         # one token: padGen, sharePct, claimable
gang creator-fee --token <addr> --claim [--to <addr>] [--part curve|pool]   # the creator only: all of it, to itself or --to
```

- Claim any time, before or after graduation, as often as wanted; there is no deadline. The fee is in the token's quote
  (WBNB, …). `--to` may be any address but 0x0 and the pad, the creator's own buy-back / split / LP module included:
  `--to <module>` puts the fee into it (the launch's `creatorFee` names the command). What pays it out from there:
  `gang split distribute` / `gang combo distribute` / `gang capped distribute` for a split module / combined / capped
  recipient (into a capped recipient, a fee in its own quote counts against the cap; in another quote it is burned). A buy-back or LP splitter
  pays its payee only when it splits: after graduation each keeper chunk does it, before graduation only
  `gang buyback split` / `gang lp split --module <splitter>` (anyone). The claim preview's `toNote` names the command
  for a module `--to`. A module splits only its own quote: when the token's quote is another one (e.g. a WBNB fee into a
  MockSPCXB splitter) the preview carries a `warning` and names `gang buyback|lp sweep --module … --asset <quote>` (all
  to the payee) or `gang split|combo distribute --module … --asset <quote>` instead.
- `gang info` shows `padGen` (`1.0` / `2.0` / `3.0` / `2.0-vault`) and, on 2.0 and 3.0, `creatorFee` (`claimable`, `claim`); `gang status` shows the
  creator its own `creatorFee`; `gang portfolio` lists the wallet's tokens with a fee waiting (`creatorFees`).
- A `create` preview on 2.0 / 3.0 carries `creatorFee`: tell the user 70% of the curve fee is theirs and how to claim it. With
  `--buyback` / `--split` / `--lp`, also tell them it does not go through that module.
- The creator can't be changed: a lost key leaves the fee on the pad for good.
- Refusals: not the creator, nothing to claim, `--to` the pad or 0x0, a 1.0 token (`GangLaunchpad 1.0, which has no
  creator fee`), a 打新 sale.

## GangLaunchpad 3.0

3.0 (CLI 0.38.0+ for new creates, 0.30.0+ for existing tokens, on BSC mainnet and the testnet) is one permanent, upgradeable pad with 2.0's
rules: the same curves, `--grad`, creator fee and 打新 sales, the same commands. What the CLI shows on top:

- `gang info` → `tokenVersion`: the rule set the token was created under (1 for 3.0's first rules). An upgrade never
  changes the rules of a token already out; new rules get a new version.
- A token bound to a lock vault (`gang create --vault`): `gang info` → `vault`, `gang status` → `lockedInVault` before
  graduation (counted in the holder's 1% cap). The CLI asks the pad whether it serves the vault mode (live on BSC
  mainnet and the testnet), so `--vault` creates on 3.0, and on the 2.0 金库版 pad only where 3.0 has no vault mode.
  Such a token takes **no bond**: `gang bond post` refuses it (see [bond.md](bond.md)); never suggest one.
- 3.0 also runs the 铸币飞轮 mode (`kind: "fly"`, [fly.md](fly.md)) since 2026-11-04.
- 2.0 and 1.0 tokens keep trading on their own pads, routers, GangBonds and modules; nothing changes for them.

### Agent window (CLI 0.49.0+)

`gang create … --agent-window <1-1440>` creates a V3 curve token that opens to every wallet that many minutes after its
create block (`openAt`). It works with any curve create (`--grad`, `--promote`, `--vault`, `--base`, `--grad-reward`, a
launch in one command), not with `--fair` / `--fly`. The CLI asks the pad first (`MAX_AGENT_WINDOW_MINUTES()`): on a pad
not upgraded for it (BSC mainnet until its upgrade) the create is refused before anything is uploaded.

- **Tell the user before they create one:** until `openAt` only Agent ID holders trade it on the curve, each under the 1%
  wallet cap; from `openAt` on **anyone** does, contracts and aggregators included, with **no 1% cap**: one buyer can take
  the rest of the curve to graduation in one block. It is **irreversible**: nobody can change, extend or cancel it. The
  preview's `agentWindow` says this; relay its `warning`.
- **Graduation reward:** once open, the pool may be taken by one large buyer (anyone can push the curve near graduation,
  then fill the list from one Agent ID). Only Agent ID wallets' buys count for the list. Relay `agentWindow.gradReward` /
  `rewards.gradReward.openWarning`.
- **Reading:** `gang info` → `agentWindow` (`set`, `openAt`, `opensIn`, `open`, `walletCap`, `status`); `gang status` adds
  `youCanTrade`. A token without one shows `set: false` (agent only for good). Buy / sell previews carry `agentWindow`;
  an open token's buy is trimmed only by the graduation cut, never by the 1% cap.

### GangSwap (CLI 0.53.0+)

`gang create … --venue gangswap [--grad 6|9|12|15] [--agent-window <min>]` creates a curve token (`kind: gangswap`) that
graduates into GangSwap instead of PancakeSwap. The CLI asks the pad first; where it is not open the create is refused.

- **Tell the user before they create one** (relay the preview's `venue`): the choice is permanent; after graduation the
  token trades on GangSwap and sites such as DexScreener may not list it. Relay the scheduled buy-back and burn and the
  fee rules shown in `venue.buyBack` and `venue.fee` ([pool.md](pool.md#gangswap-pools)).
- The GangSwap version is fixed at create. `venue.protocolVersion` gives its name and `venue.version` is the migrator the create names. If the pad moves
  to another version first, the create reverts (`GangSwapVersionChanged`): re-run, show the new version, ask again.
  `gang tx` shows `gangSwap.migrator`, the one the token got, for good.
- `warning` / `venue.routerWarning`: this CLI version has no router for that version (it could not trade the token after
  graduation), or that could not be read. Relay it and update the CLI before creating.
- WBNB quote, untaxed, alone: no tax, dividend, recipient, other quote, promoter share, base, vault, module, countdown reward, bond,
  call bond or launch in one command; not with `--fair` / `--fly`. `gang tx --hash` on the create shows `gangSwap.verified`
  and the token's creator.
- **Graduation reward** (0.54.0+, only where the pad serves it: the CLI asks before any wrap or approval, and refuses with
  `no graduation reward for GangSwap tokens yet` elsewhere; mainnet does not serve it yet): `--grad-reward <seed>` with
  `--gr-min-buy`, `--gr-entries`, `--gr-last`, `--gr-refund-days`, `--grad`, `--agent-window`. The creator's own WBNB, the
  same rules, claims and refund as any graduation reward. One step per run of the same command: `wrap-bnb`,
  `approve-quote` (exactly the seed, only if the approval is short), then the create; relay `gradReward` and `venue` at each. A larger approval given before is left as
  it is: relay `gradReward.allowance` (and `funds` when a create is refused after funding). `--gr-carry`, `--promote` and
  `--countdown-reward` are refused; the unclaimed rest goes back to the creator. The seed is never part of the GangSwap
  pool or its buy-back. `gang info`, `gang buy` / `gang sell` (`rewards.gradReward`) and `gang grad-reward …` work on it.
- On the curve it is an ordinary V3 token (same commands, fee, 1% cap, agent gate and window, notes and follows).
- **Creator fee:** `gang creator-fee --token <addr> --claim` can build separate claims for the curve and pool.
  `--part pool` selects the pool. v1 pays the creator 70% of WBNB buy fees. v2 pays the creator 70% of WBNB buy and
  sell fees after the fee window closes. A v2 pool claim pays 0 WBNB during the window or on the closing collection.
- `gang info` / `gang status` show the version, fee rules, effective and real liquidity, buy-back progress and the v2
  fee window under `gangSwap`.

### Promoter share (CLI 0.34.0+)

A dev can give promoters a share of the creator fee at create: `gang create … --promote <0.01-50> [--promote-days <7-90>]`.
It is fixed for good, on V3 curve tokens of an upgraded 3.0 pad only.

- **Invite:** `https://gang.fun/token/<addr>?promoter=0x…` means `gang promote buy --token <addr> --amount <n> --promoter 0x…`.
  The wallet binds by its first trade of the token, sent directly from the wallet, and only then. Same price and fee for
  the buyer. It is not `--ref`, which is gang.fun's invite credit.
- **A plain first buy loses it:** the preview of a plain `gang buy` carries `promoteWarning` when this could happen; relay it.
- **If it is refused:** say why and stop. An RPC error (`retry`) means run the same command again, not a plain buy.
- **Income:** `gang promote status --token <addr>`, `gang promote earnings`, `gang promote claim`, `gang promote claim-pool`.
  On a taxed token whose recipient is a promoter pool module, it continues in the pool under the limits `status` and
  the create's preview state. Pool income waits for a settler's root and a 2-day dispute window.

## Reading results

- `create` preview: `split` spells out who gets what of each pool tax; `recipientLabel` is the whitelist name (`you
  (the creator)` for the creating wallet). Read the rates, the split and the recipient to the user before they
  confirm (they are permanent), and say the tax starts only in the pool after graduation.
- `buy`: `spend` includes the 1% fee (`curveTaxPct: 0`; `poolBuyTaxPct` is what applies after graduation). At the
  wallet cap (or the graduation cut) the excess is refunded in the quote; a BNB buy through the router gets it back as
  WBNB. When the pad will trim the buy, the preview carries `spent` (fee included), `refund`, `cutBy` (`graduation`:
  only `toGraduation` is left before the token's own graduation amount, which on 2.0 / 3.0 follows its picked target, never
  a fixed 15 / 9 BNB; `wallet cap`: the 1% cap) and `cutNote`; tell the user what the buy really spends.
- `sell`: `expectOut` is what arrives after the 1% fee (`poolSellTaxPct` applies only in the pool).
- `info.taxNote`: the tax setting in words. `info.owedToRecipient` > 0: a payment to the recipient failed (e.g. the
  quote blocks that address); the trade went through and `gang release` sends it once the recipient accepts the quote.
  `info.taxHandler`: the contract that splits the tax and keeps the whitelist and the owed payments.
- `status.dividend`: `claimable` can be claimed now; `creditedUnsoldEstimate` was credited when the tax was paid and
  becomes claimable once that tax is sold (valued at the current price). `nothing to claim yet` with a
  `creditedUnsoldEstimate` means wait for the next sells.
- `create` failing with `VaultFloorTooHigh`: the platform's dividend vault can't take a token with `--dividend` > 0
  right now; `--dividend 0` works.
- Errors `Untaxed` (an untaxed token has no tax, route or dividend), `WrongKind` (the other kind's command),
  `NotReclaimable` / `NotLocked` (打新-only actions) come with a `hint`: follow it.

## Buy-back splitter (`--buyback`)

A dev who wants part of its tax bought back gives `--buyback <0-100>` on a taxed launch (`gang create … --buy-tax …
--sell-tax … --buyback 50`, see [create.md](create.md#launch-in-one-command---dev-buy---bond---buyback---split---lp)). The recipient
becomes the dev's own GangBuybackSplitter. Each payment to it is split: `--buyback` % is bought back in the PancakeSwap
pool and sent to 0xdead (a normal pool buy that pays the buy tax), the rest goes to the dev, or to `--buyback-to <addr>`
(the payee; the dev can change it at once). The keeper buys back in chunks (≤ 0.5% of the pool's quote side per block,
none while the price is > 2% above its 10-minute average). `gang info --token …` shows it under `buyback`.

```bash
gang buyback status --token 0x…                 # ratio, a cut asked for, quote waiting, the payee and what it is owed, the next chunk
gang buyback set --token 0x… --pct 60           # dev only: a raise applies at once; a cut after a 3-day notice
gang buyback set-payee --token 0x… --to 0x…     # dev only, at once (the first mainnet splitters, buyback-burn-v1, always pay the dev)
gang buyback apply --token 0x…                  # anyone: apply a cut whose notice ran (within 7 days, or it lapses)
gang buyback bind --token 0x… | gang buyback release --token 0x…   # anyone: bind (the launch does it) / push what a failed payment left owed
gang buyback split --token 0x… (or --module 0x…)   # anyone: split what arrived: the payee's part now, the buy-back's set aside
gang buyback sweep --token 0x… (or --module 0x…) --asset 0x…|wbnb   # anyone: an asset that is not its quote, all to the payee
```

## Split module (`--split`)

A dev who wants the recipient's part of the tax shared gives `--split self:60,0xKol…:30,0xDao…:10` on a taxed launch
([create.md](create.md#launch-in-one-command---dev-buy---bond---buyback---split---lp)). The recipient becomes the dev's own
GangSplitModule (`split-v1`): everything it receives is split among 1–10 payees at the shares given. **The payees and
shares are fixed for good**: nobody (not the dev, not the platform) can change them. A payee can only move its own slot
to another address (`set-payee`; the share stays). Slots are the contract's 0-based index (`--index`), in the order
given; the first payee also takes the rounding dust. `gang info --token …` shows it under `split`.

- **Who splits.** The keeper splits the bound quote when it serves it (WBNB, GANG, a QuoteRegistry quote) once enough
  has gathered (0.05% of the pool's quote reserve, or after a day at 1/100 of that); `status` says `distribution:
  keeper`. Any other quote (`distribution: manual`), and any other asset sent to the module, is split only by hand:
  `gang split distribute` (anyone). `status` lists what waits and the commands in `next`.
- **Owed.** A push that fails (a payee that cannot receive) is owed to that slot and paid with its next push, or by
  `gang split release` (anyone).
- **Gas.** Never set or lower a gas limit on distribute / release / set-payee / the keeper's work: each payee's push
  needs ~365k gas left before it, so the right limit is far above the gas used. Let the wallet estimate it; a lower
  limit reverts `PushStarved` (nothing moves).
- **Another token paying the module.** If `status` / `info` shows `warning` (the module is bound to another token), the
  keeper never splits this token's tax: say so, and split it by hand.

```bash
gang split status --token 0x…                              # payees + shares, bound token / quote, what waits, what each slot is owed, keeper due?
gang split distribute --token 0x… [--asset 0x…|wbnb]       # anyone: split what arrived (default the bound quote)
gang split release --token 0x… [--asset 0x…] [--index <i>] [--max <amount>]   # anyone: push a slot what it is owed
gang split set-payee --token 0x… --to 0x… [--index <i>]    # only that slot's payee (default: your own slot); splits waiting WBNB / GANG first (re-run)
gang split bind --token 0x…                                # anyone: bind (the launch does it)
```

## LP splitter (`--lp`)

A dev who wants part of its tax to deepen the pool gives `--lp <0-100>` on a taxed launch (`gang create … --buy-tax …
--sell-tax … --lp 70`, see [create.md](create.md#launch-in-one-command---dev-buy---bond---buyback---split---lp)). The
recipient becomes the dev's own GangLiquiditySplitter (`lp-burn-v1`). Each payment to it is split as the buy-back
splitter's is: `--lp` % is set aside for the pool (`lpLeft`), the rest goes to the dev, or to `--lp-to <addr>` (the
payee; the dev can change it at once). `gang info --token …` shows it under `lp`.

- **The chunks.** From graduation on the keeper adds the set-aside quote in chunks (one per 4 minutes, at most 0.5% of
  the pair's quote reserve, none while the price is > 2% above its 10-minute average; it waits until at least 0.05%
  of that reserve is set aside): half buys the token in the pool, all of it goes back into the pair with the matching
  quote, and the pair mints the LP to 0xdead, locked for good like the graduation LP. Both pool taxes apply (the buy
  and the deposit), so part of each chunk's quote stays for the next. `status` shows the next chunk (`nextChunk`) or
  what it waits for (`nextChunkWaitsFor`).
- **By hand.** `gang lp add` (anyone) adds one chunk now, with a minimum of the previewed tokens less `--slippage`
  (default 1%). When the price check is not ready it builds a call that only records the price (`step:
  record-price`, `addsLiquidity: false`): say so.
- **Gas.** A chunk takes about 0.53–0.75M gas (the deposit lets the tax handler sell a chunk of its tax first). Never
  set or trim a gas limit on `add`: let the wallet estimate it.
- **Ratio and payee.** As `gang buyback`: a raise applies at once, a cut 3 days after it is asked for (then anyone
  applies it within 7 days, or it lapses); the payee changes at once.

```bash
gang lp status --token 0x…                 # ratio, a cut asked for, lpLeft / unsplit, the payee and what it is owed, the next chunk
gang lp set --token 0x… --pct 80           # dev only: a raise applies at once; a cut after a 3-day notice
gang lp set-payee --token 0x… --to 0x…     # dev only, at once
gang lp apply --token 0x…                  # anyone: apply a cut whose notice ran (within 7 days, or it lapses)
gang lp bind --token 0x… | gang lp release --token 0x…   # anyone: bind (the launch does it) / push what a failed payment left owed
gang lp split --token 0x… (or --module 0x…)   # anyone: split what arrived: the payee's part now, the pool's set aside
gang lp sweep --token 0x… (or --module 0x…) --asset 0x…|wbnb   # anyone: an asset that is not its quote, all to the payee
gang lp add --token 0x… [--slippage 1]     # anyone: one chunk now (graduated tokens; one per 4 minutes)
```

## Combined recipient (several of `--buyback`, `--lp`, `--split`)

Two or three of `--buyback`, `--lp`, `--split` on one taxed launch (`gang create … --buy-tax … --sell-tax … --buyback 30
--lp 30 --split self:70,0xKol…:30`, see [create.md](create.md#launch-in-one-command---dev-buy---bond---buyback---split---lp))
make the recipient one GangComboSplitter (`combo-v1`). Of each payment of the token's quote to it, `--buyback` % is set
aside for buy-back-and-burn (`buybackLeft`), `--lp` % for liquidity (`lpLeft`), and the rest is pushed to the payees at
their shares of that rest (without `--split`: the dev alone; at `--buyback` + `--lp` = 100: no payees). `gang info
--token …` shows it under `combo`.

- **Permanent.** The ratios, payees and shares are fixed when it is cloned: nobody (not the dev, not the platform) can
  change them, unlike a lone buy-back or LP splitter whose dev can change its ratio. A payee can only move its own slot
  (`gang combo set-payee`, at once; the share stays with the slot). Say so before the launch.
- **The chunks.** From graduation on: one chunk per 4 minutes for both kinds together, always of the larger set-aside
  part (a buy-back as the buy-back splitter does it, or a liquidity add as the LP splitter does it), at most 0.5% of the
  pair's quote reserve, none while the price is > 2% above its 10-minute average. `status` shows the next chunk
  (`nextChunk.kind`, ready or `nextChunkWaitsFor`) and the keeper's plan: `previewWork` encodes the action in its
  `minOut`'s top byte (1 buy-back, 2 liquidity, 3 price observation, 4 split that pays the payees); the CLI decodes it
  as `keeper.plan`.
- **By hand.** `gang combo chunk` (anyone) runs one chunk now with a minimum of the previewed tokens less `--slippage`
  (default 1%); when the price check is not ready it builds a call that only records the price (`step: record-price`).
  `gang combo distribute` (anyone, once bound) splits the bound quote that arrived; for **another asset** (`--asset`)
  the buy-back + LP share goes to 0xdead and the rest to the payees (all of it to 0xdead without payees). Nothing is
  split before `bind` (`NotBound`).
- **Decoy bind.** If the token paying the module is not the one it is bound to, `status` / `info` carry a `warning`:
  the buy-backs and liquidity go to the bound token's pair, and this token's tax is only distributed by hand.
- **Gas.** A chunk takes about 0.55–0.75M gas, and a split pushes up to 10 payees (each push needs about 365k gas left
  before it). Never set or trim a gas limit: let the wallet estimate it; a lower one reverts `PushStarved`.
- **Where.** BSC mainnet and testnet (set 10).

```bash
gang combo status --token 0x… [--asset 0x…]   # ratios, payees, buybackLeft / lpLeft / unsplit, owed, next chunk, keeper plan, decoy warning
gang combo distribute --token 0x… [--asset 0x…|wbnb]   # anyone: split the bound quote, or another asset (buy-back + LP share to 0xdead)
gang combo release --token 0x… [--asset 0x…] [--index <i>] [--max <amount>]   # anyone: push a slot what a failed push left owed
gang combo set-payee --token 0x… --to 0x… [--index <i>]   # only that slot's payee (default: your own slot)
gang combo bind --token 0x…                    # anyone: bind (the launch does it)
gang combo chunk --token 0x… [--slippage 1]    # anyone: one chunk now (graduated tokens; one per 4 minutes for both kinds)
```

## Capped recipient (`--tax-cap`)

`gang create … --buy-tax … --sell-tax … --tax-cap 20 [--tax-cap-share 50] [--split self:70,0xKol…:30] [--after-cap buyback:70,lp:30]`
(CLI 0.35.0+, see [create.md](create.md#launch-in-one-command---dev-buy---bond---buyback---split---lp)) makes the
recipient one GangCappedSplitter (`capped-v1`). Of each payment of the token's quote to it, `--tax-cap-share` % (default
100) is pushed to the payees at their shares until they have been credited `--tax-cap` in all (`paidTotal`, counted when
split, pushed or owed alike; the split that crosses the cap pays exactly what is left of it). The rest, and once the
cap is reached all of it, is set aside: `--after-cap`'s buyback % for buy-back-and-burn (`buybackLeft`), the remainder for
liquidity (`lpLeft`). `gang info --token …` shows it under `capped`.

- **The cap.** Counted in `capQuote`, the launch's quote, in its own decimals (the CLI reads them; never assume 18),
  for all payees together. `bind` takes only a token quoted in `capQuote` (`WrongQuote`), and the module takes only a
  quote the keeper serves (`BadQuote`: WBNB, GANG or a QuoteRegistry quote). Roughly, the cap is reached at
  cap / ((tax − 0.5%) × (1 − dividend) × cap share) of pool volume: 3% tax, 40% dividend, 50% share and a 20 BNB cap ≈
  2,667 BNB. `status` shows `cap.paid`, `cap.left`, `cap.progressPct` and `cap.reached`.
- **What it covers.** Only this recipient's part of the pool tax. Not the creator's share of the curve fee (on the pad,
  claimed apart: `gang creator-fee`), not the dev's own tokens (it is not a lock: the dev can sell), not the holders'
  dividend. Say so before the launch, in these words.
- **Permanent.** The cap, its quote, the ratios, payees and shares are fixed when it is cloned; a payee can only move
  its own slot (`gang capped set-payee`), which leaves the cap as it is.
- **Other assets.** Anything but the bound quote can't count against the cap: `gang capped distribute --asset …` sends
  all of it to 0xdead (none to the payees; one that can't be sent there stays in the module). Payees are owed only the
  bound quote (`release` of another asset is refused).
- **Chunks, keeper, gas.** As the combined recipient's: one chunk per 4 minutes, `keeper.plan` decoded from
  `previewWork`, `gang capped chunk` by hand; never set a gas limit.
- **Decoy bind.** If the token paying the module is not the one it is bound to, `status` / `info` carry a `warning`:
  in the same quote its tax is split under the one cap and bought back in the other token's pair; in another quote it
  is all burned.
- **Where.** BSC mainnet (factory `0x294E901ecb66af280C1cDD26fD871601d89a2Dca`, 2026-11-08) and the BSC testnet
  (`0xe3f61865d98993dd39c136Af19651C6F6686498A`), both built in.

```bash
gang capped status --token 0x… [--asset 0x…]   # cap, paid / left / progress, ratios, payees, set-aside parts, owed, next chunk, keeper plan, decoy warning
gang capped distribute --token 0x… [--asset 0x…|wbnb]   # anyone: split the bound quote (payees up to the cap), or send another asset to 0xdead
gang capped release --token 0x… [--index <i>] [--max <amount>]   # anyone: push a slot what a failed push left owed (bound quote)
gang capped set-payee --token 0x… --to 0x… [--index <i>]   # only that slot's payee (default: your own slot)
gang capped bind --token 0x…                    # anyone: bind (the launch does it)
gang capped chunk --token 0x… [--slippage 1]    # anyone: one chunk now (graduated tokens; one per 4 minutes for both kinds)
```

## Countdown reward (`--countdown-reward`, 0.43.2+)

倒计时奖励 (GangCountdownReward, `countdown-v1`): the token's tax recipient is its own module, and the recipient's part
of the pool tax becomes a reward pool for buyers. Say it in these words: the creator's tax share becomes buyers' rewards,
paid by a fixed public rule. Buyers pay the pool price and the buy tax, nothing extra, and keep the tokens they buy.

- **Rules.** After graduation each buy of at least `--cd-min-buy` through the module puts the countdown `--cd-window`
  ahead (default 60m), never past `--cd-max-round` (6h) from the round's first counted buy. When it runs out, the
  round's last `--cd-entries` (10) counted buys share `--cd-payout` % (30) of the pool, at most `--cd-max-payout`
  (required): equal shares, the newest `--cd-last` % (20) on top. A round that ends at its cap pays equal shares only.
  No round starts under `--cd-min-pot`; after `--cd-idle` days (30) with no counted buy anyone may `finalize`.
  `--cd-dev` % (0) of the arriving tax stays with the creator. Minimum buy / pool default to 0.05 / 0.1 BNB on a
  WBNB-quoted token; another quote needs them given. Every setting is fixed for good.
- **Launch.** Like `--floor`: the token's address first, the module cloned for it, the create at that address, bind,
  then `--cd-seed` (approve, fund; a fund that doesn't show in the module's books is built again only with
  `--cd-seed-again`: the first may still wait for approval in the Binance App, approve only one). One tx per run; a changed tax rate or `--cd-*` setting is refused. Not with
  `--floor`, `--buyback`, `--lp`, `--split`, `--tax-cap`, `--recipient`, `--vault` or (for now) `--grad-reward`; `--dividend` below 100.
- **Honest notes** (the preview carries them; relay them): anyone, the creator included, can take slots with several
  wallets; a round that ends at its cap has a public end time, so its last slots favour whoever controls ordering; only
  buys through the module count; there is no holding check and sells don't take out slots.
- **Buys.** `gang buy` on a graduated countdown token goes through the module with `mustCount` (see trade.md);
  `rewards.countdown` carries whether it counts and why, the round, its deadline and cap, the pool and an estimated
  share. Curve buys never count.
- **Provenance.** Only a module that GangCountdownRewardFactory listed (GangHandlerAdmin.moduleOf) and cloned for this
  very token is shown with its numbers or bought through; anything else gets a warning only.
- **Where.** BSC mainnet (factory `0xa1e3117a59104de7D97667cF22f9AB886a5f2957`, 2026-11-17) and the BSC testnet (`0x0f83ABC7d39C0d043d3f5612E304D9395B5fe582`), both built in.

```bash
gang countdown status --token 0x… [--from 0x…]   # rules, pool, round (deadline, cap, slots), what is due, claimable
gang countdown claim --token 0x… [--to 0x…]      # a buyer: everything credited to this wallet
gang countdown settle --token 0x…                # anyone, once a round passed its deadline
gang countdown finalize --token 0x…              # anyone, after the idle days with no counted buy
gang countdown claim-dev --token 0x…             # anyone: the creator's --cd-dev part, always to the creator
```
