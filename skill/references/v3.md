# V3 tokens and the pool tax

A V3 token is GangLaunchpad's bonding-curve token: Agent-only curve, 1% wallet cap, graduates at ≈ 15 BNB of its quote
into a PancakeSwap V2 pool (LP burned). `gang create` makes one ([create.md](create.md)). On the curve every V3 token
pays **only the 1% fee, no tax**. After graduation it trades in the pool ([pool.md](pool.md)).

**Untaxed** (the default): no `--buy-tax` / `--sell-tax`. No pool tax, no dividend, no recipient (`--dividend` /
`--recipient` without the tax flags are refused).

**Taxed**: `--buy-tax` and `--sell-tax`, 1–5% each, set separately and fixed at create, charged **only in the pool**:
- The platform keeps **0.5% of trade volume** of every pool tax (a 2% tax: 0.5% platform, 1.5% shared out).
- `--dividend 0-100`: that % of the rest goes to holders (holding ≥ 10,000 tokens, by balance); the rest goes to
  `--recipient` **in the quote token** (BNB → WBNB, USDT, …), never in the token. If no holder has 10,000+ tokens when
  a tax is paid, the holders' part goes to the recipient.
- `--recipient` defaults to the creating wallet (no whitelist needed), or a partner on the platform's whitelist (e.g. a
  partner's treasury contract). Any other address is refused (`recipient not listed`). It can't be changed later. Only
  `--dividend 100` may leave it out. For a recipient that buys part of the tax back, see
  [Buy-back splitter](#buy-back-splitter---buyback).
- Dividend shares are tracked from the curve on (by balance), so curve buyers who still hold at graduation earn from
  the pool tax. A holder's share of a tax is credited to whoever holds when it is paid, never to the trader for its own
  trade's tax.
- The closest to LaunchpadV2's old 1% taxed track: `--buy-tax 1 --sell-tax 1 --dividend 100`.

```bash
gang info --token <addr>               # curve, tax setting (taxNote), recipient (+ whitelist name), what is owed to it; graduated: swapBack
gang status --token <addr>             # my balance; dividend: claimable + credited, not sold yet (estimate); graduated: poolSellAll
gang claim --token <addr>              # claim my dividend (paid in the quote)
gang release --token <addr>            # push an owed payment to the recipient (the caller gets nothing)
gang v3 income --recipient <addr>      # what a recipient received, per token (from gang.fun)
```

## Reading results

- `create` preview: `split` spells out who gets what of each pool tax; `recipientLabel` is the whitelist name (`you
  (the creator)` for the creating wallet). Read the rates, the split and the recipient to the user before they
  confirm (they are permanent), and say the tax starts only in the pool after graduation.
- `buy`: `spend` includes the 1% fee (`curveTaxPct: 0`; `poolBuyTaxPct` is what applies after graduation). At the
  wallet cap (or the graduation cut) the excess is refunded in the quote; a BNB buy through the router gets it back as
  WBNB.
- `sell`: `expectOut` is what arrives after the 1% fee (`poolSellTaxPct` applies only in the pool).
- `info.taxNote`: the tax setting in words. `info.owedToRecipient` > 0: a payment to the recipient failed (e.g. the
  quote blocks that address); the trade went through and `gang release` sends it once the recipient accepts the quote.
  `info.taxHandler`: the contract that splits the tax and keeps the whitelist and the owed payments.
- `status.dividend`: `claimable` can be claimed now; `creditedUnsoldEstimate` was credited when the tax was paid and
  becomes claimable once that tax is sold (valued at the current price). `nothing to claim yet` with a
  `creditedUnsoldEstimate` means wait for the next sells.
- `create` failing with `VaultFloorTooHigh`: the platform's dividend vault can't take a token with `--dividend` > 0
  right now; `--dividend 0` works.
- Errors `Untaxed` (an untaxed token has no tax, route or dividend), `WrongKind` (the other kind's command),
  `NotReclaimable` / `NotLocked` (打新-only actions) come with a `hint`: follow it.

## Buy-back splitter (`--buyback`)

A dev who wants part of its tax bought back gives `--buyback <0-100>` on a taxed launch (`gang create … --buy-tax …
--sell-tax … --buyback 50`, see [create.md](create.md#launch-in-one-command---dev-buy---bond---buyback)). The recipient
becomes the dev's own GangBuybackSplitter. Each payment to it is split: `--buyback` % is bought back in the PancakeSwap
pool and sent to 0xdead (a normal pool buy that pays the buy tax), the rest goes to the dev, or to `--buyback-to <addr>`
(the payee; the dev can change it at once). The keeper buys back in chunks (≤ 0.5% of the pool's quote side per block,
none while the price is > 2% above its 10-minute average). `gang info --token …` shows it under `buyback`.

```bash
gang buyback status --token 0x…                 # ratio, a cut asked for, quote waiting, the payee and what it is owed, the next chunk
gang buyback set --token 0x… --pct 60           # dev only: a raise applies at once; a cut after a 3-day notice
gang buyback set-payee --token 0x… --to 0x…     # dev only, at once (the first mainnet splitters, buyback-burn-v1, always pay the dev)
gang buyback apply --token 0x…                  # anyone: apply a cut whose notice ran (within 7 days, or it lapses)
gang buyback bind --token 0x… | gang buyback release --token 0x…   # anyone: bind (the launch does it) / push what a failed payment left owed
```
