# V3 tokens and the pool tax

A V3 token is GangLaunchpad's bonding-curve token: Agent-only curve, 1% wallet cap, graduates at ≈ 15 BNB of its quote
into a PancakeSwap V2 pool (LP burned). `gang create` makes one ([create.md](create.md)). On the curve every V3 token
pays **only the 1% fee, no tax**. After graduation it trades in the pool ([pool.md](pool.md)).

**Untaxed** (the default): no `--buy-tax` / `--sell-tax`. No pool tax, no dividend, no recipient (`--dividend` /
`--recipient` without the tax flags are refused).

**Taxed**: `--buy-tax` and `--sell-tax`, 1–5% each, set separately and fixed at create, charged **only in the pool**:
- The platform keeps **0.5% of trade volume** of every pool tax (a 2% tax: 0.5% platform, 1.5% shared out).
- `--dividend 0-100`: that % of the rest goes to holders (holding ≥ 10,000 tokens, by balance); the rest goes to
  `--recipient` **in the quote token** (BNB → WBNB, USDT, …), never in the token. If no holder has 10,000+ tokens when
  a tax is paid, the holders' part goes to the recipient.
- `--recipient` defaults to the creating wallet (no whitelist needed), or a partner on the platform's whitelist (e.g. a
  partner's treasury contract). Any other address is refused (`recipient not listed`). It can't be changed later. Only
  `--dividend 100` may leave it out. For a recipient that buys part of the tax back, see
  [Buy-back splitter](#buy-back-splitter---buyback); for one that splits it among several payees, see
  [Split module](#split-module---split); for one that adds part of it to the pool as locked liquidity, see
  [LP splitter](#lp-splitter---lp); for several of these at once, see
  [Combined recipient](#combined-recipient-several-of---buyback---lp---split).
- Dividend shares are tracked from the curve on (by balance), so curve buyers who still hold at graduation earn from
  the pool tax. A holder's share of a tax is credited to whoever holds when it is paid, never to the trader for its own
  trade's tax.
- The closest to LaunchpadV2's old 1% taxed track: `--buy-tax 1 --sell-tax 1 --dividend 100`.

```bash
gang info --token <addr>               # curve, tax setting (taxNote), recipient (+ whitelist name), what is owed to it; graduated: swapBack
gang status --token <addr>             # my balance; dividend: claimable + credited, not sold yet (estimate); graduated: poolSellAll
gang claim --token <addr>              # claim my dividend (paid in the quote)
gang release --token <addr>            # push an owed payment to the recipient (the caller gets nothing)
gang v3 income --recipient <addr>      # what a recipient received, per token (from gang.fun)
```

## Reading results

- `create` preview: `split` spells out who gets what of each pool tax; `recipientLabel` is the whitelist name (`you
  (the creator)` for the creating wallet). Read the rates, the split and the recipient to the user before they
  confirm (they are permanent), and say the tax starts only in the pool after graduation.
- `buy`: `spend` includes the 1% fee (`curveTaxPct: 0`; `poolBuyTaxPct` is what applies after graduation). At the
  wallet cap (or the graduation cut) the excess is refunded in the quote; a BNB buy through the router gets it back as
  WBNB.
- `sell`: `expectOut` is what arrives after the 1% fee (`poolSellTaxPct` applies only in the pool).
- `info.taxNote`: the tax setting in words. `info.owedToRecipient` > 0: a payment to the recipient failed (e.g. the
  quote blocks that address); the trade went through and `gang release` sends it once the recipient accepts the quote.
  `info.taxHandler`: the contract that splits the tax and keeps the whitelist and the owed payments.
- `status.dividend`: `claimable` can be claimed now; `creditedUnsoldEstimate` was credited when the tax was paid and
  becomes claimable once that tax is sold (valued at the current price). `nothing to claim yet` with a
  `creditedUnsoldEstimate` means wait for the next sells.
- `create` failing with `VaultFloorTooHigh`: the platform's dividend vault can't take a token with `--dividend` > 0
  right now; `--dividend 0` works.
- Errors `Untaxed` (an untaxed token has no tax, route or dividend), `WrongKind` (the other kind's command),
  `NotReclaimable` / `NotLocked` (打新-only actions) come with a `hint`: follow it.

## Buy-back splitter (`--buyback`)

A dev who wants part of its tax bought back gives `--buyback <0-100>` on a taxed launch (`gang create … --buy-tax …
--sell-tax … --buyback 50`, see [create.md](create.md#launch-in-one-command---dev-buy---bond---buyback---split---lp)). The recipient
becomes the dev's own GangBuybackSplitter. Each payment to it is split: `--buyback` % is bought back in the PancakeSwap
pool and sent to 0xdead (a normal pool buy that pays the buy tax), the rest goes to the dev, or to `--buyback-to <addr>`
(the payee; the dev can change it at once). The keeper buys back in chunks (≤ 0.5% of the pool's quote side per block,
none while the price is > 2% above its 10-minute average). `gang info --token …` shows it under `buyback`.

```bash
gang buyback status --token 0x…                 # ratio, a cut asked for, quote waiting, the payee and what it is owed, the next chunk
gang buyback set --token 0x… --pct 60           # dev only: a raise applies at once; a cut after a 3-day notice
gang buyback set-payee --token 0x… --to 0x…     # dev only, at once (the first mainnet splitters, buyback-burn-v1, always pay the dev)
gang buyback apply --token 0x…                  # anyone: apply a cut whose notice ran (within 7 days, or it lapses)
gang buyback bind --token 0x… | gang buyback release --token 0x…   # anyone: bind (the launch does it) / push what a failed payment left owed
```

## Split module (`--split`)

A dev who wants the recipient's part of the tax shared gives `--split self:60,0xKol…:30,0xDao…:10` on a taxed launch
([create.md](create.md#launch-in-one-command---dev-buy---bond---buyback---split---lp)). The recipient becomes the dev's own
GangSplitModule (`split-v1`): everything it receives is split among 1–10 payees at the shares given. **The payees and
shares are fixed for good**: nobody (not the dev, not the platform) can change them. A payee can only move its own slot
to another address (`set-payee`; the share stays). Slots are the contract's 0-based index (`--index`), in the order
given; the first payee also takes the rounding dust. `gang info --token …` shows it under `split`.

- **Who splits.** The keeper splits the bound quote when it serves it (WBNB, GANG, a QuoteRegistry quote) once enough
  has gathered (0.05% of the pool's quote reserve, or after a day at 1/100 of that); `status` says `distribution:
  keeper`. Any other quote (`distribution: manual`), and any other asset sent to the module, is split only by hand:
  `gang split distribute` (anyone). `status` lists what waits and the commands in `next`.
- **Owed.** A push that fails (a payee that cannot receive) is owed to that slot and paid with its next push, or by
  `gang split release` (anyone).
- **Gas.** Never set or lower a gas limit on distribute / release / set-payee / the keeper's work: each payee's push
  needs ~365k gas left before it, so the right limit is far above the gas used. Let the wallet estimate it; a lower
  limit reverts `PushStarved` (nothing moves).
- **Another token paying the module.** If `status` / `info` shows `warning` (the module is bound to another token), the
  keeper never splits this token's tax: say so, and split it by hand.

```bash
gang split status --token 0x…                              # payees + shares, bound token / quote, what waits, what each slot is owed, keeper due?
gang split distribute --token 0x… [--asset 0x…|wbnb]       # anyone: split what arrived (default the bound quote)
gang split release --token 0x… [--asset 0x…] [--index <i>] [--max <amount>]   # anyone: push a slot what it is owed
gang split set-payee --token 0x… --to 0x… [--index <i>]    # only that slot's payee (default: your own slot); splits waiting WBNB / GANG first (re-run)
gang split bind --token 0x…                                # anyone: bind (the launch does it)
```

## LP splitter (`--lp`)

A dev who wants part of its tax to deepen the pool gives `--lp <0-100>` on a taxed launch (`gang create … --buy-tax …
--sell-tax … --lp 70`, see [create.md](create.md#launch-in-one-command---dev-buy---bond---buyback---split---lp)). The
recipient becomes the dev's own GangLiquiditySplitter (`lp-burn-v1`). Each payment to it is split as the buy-back
splitter's is: `--lp` % is set aside for the pool (`lpLeft`), the rest goes to the dev, or to `--lp-to <addr>` (the
payee; the dev can change it at once). `gang info --token …` shows it under `lp`.

- **The chunks.** From graduation on the keeper adds the set-aside quote in chunks (one per 4 minutes, at most 0.5% of
  the pair's quote reserve, none while the price is > 2% above its 10-minute average; it waits until at least 0.05%
  of that reserve is set aside): half buys the token in the pool, all of it goes back into the pair with the matching
  quote, and the pair mints the LP to 0xdead, locked for good like the graduation LP. Both pool taxes apply (the buy
  and the deposit), so part of each chunk's quote stays for the next. `status` shows the next chunk (`nextChunk`) or
  what it waits for (`nextChunkWaitsFor`).
- **By hand.** `gang lp add` (anyone) adds one chunk now, with a minimum of the previewed tokens less `--slippage`
  (default 1%). When the price check is not ready it builds a call that only records the price (`step:
  record-price`, `addsLiquidity: false`): say so.
- **Gas.** A chunk takes about 0.53–0.75M gas (the deposit lets the tax handler sell a chunk of its tax first). Never
  set or trim a gas limit on `add`: let the wallet estimate it.
- **Ratio and payee.** As `gang buyback`: a raise applies at once, a cut 3 days after it is asked for (then anyone
  applies it within 7 days, or it lapses); the payee changes at once.

```bash
gang lp status --token 0x…                 # ratio, a cut asked for, lpLeft / unsplit, the payee and what it is owed, the next chunk
gang lp set --token 0x… --pct 80           # dev only: a raise applies at once; a cut after a 3-day notice
gang lp set-payee --token 0x… --to 0x…     # dev only, at once
gang lp apply --token 0x…                  # anyone: apply a cut whose notice ran (within 7 days, or it lapses)
gang lp bind --token 0x… | gang lp release --token 0x…   # anyone: bind (the launch does it) / push what a failed payment left owed
gang lp add --token 0x… [--slippage 1]     # anyone: one chunk now (graduated tokens; one per 4 minutes)
```

## Combined recipient (several of `--buyback`, `--lp`, `--split`)

Two or three of `--buyback`, `--lp`, `--split` on one taxed launch (`gang create … --buy-tax … --sell-tax … --buyback 30
--lp 30 --split self:70,0xKol…:30`, see [create.md](create.md#launch-in-one-command---dev-buy---bond---buyback---split---lp))
make the recipient one GangComboSplitter (`combo-v1`). Of each payment of the token's quote to it, `--buyback` % is set
aside for buy-back-and-burn (`buybackLeft`), `--lp` % for liquidity (`lpLeft`), and the rest is pushed to the payees at
their shares of that rest (without `--split`: the dev alone; at `--buyback` + `--lp` = 100: no payees). `gang info
--token …` shows it under `combo`.

- **Permanent.** The ratios, payees and shares are fixed when it is cloned: nobody (not the dev, not the platform) can
  change them, unlike a lone buy-back or LP splitter whose dev can change its ratio. A payee can only move its own slot
  (`gang combo set-payee`, at once; the share stays with the slot). Say so before the launch.
- **The chunks.** From graduation on: one chunk per 4 minutes for both kinds together, always of the larger set-aside
  part (a buy-back as the buy-back splitter does it, or a liquidity add as the LP splitter does it), at most 0.5% of the
  pair's quote reserve, none while the price is > 2% above its 10-minute average. `status` shows the next chunk
  (`nextChunk.kind`, ready or `nextChunkWaitsFor`) and the keeper's plan: `previewWork` encodes the action in its
  `minOut`'s top byte (1 buy-back, 2 liquidity, 3 price observation, 4 split that pays the payees); the CLI decodes it
  as `keeper.plan`.
- **By hand.** `gang combo chunk` (anyone) runs one chunk now with a minimum of the previewed tokens less `--slippage`
  (default 1%); when the price check is not ready it builds a call that only records the price (`step: record-price`).
  `gang combo distribute` (anyone, once bound) splits the bound quote that arrived; for **another asset** (`--asset`)
  the buy-back + LP share goes to 0xdead and the rest to the payees (all of it to 0xdead without payees). Nothing is
  split before `bind` (`NotBound`).
- **Decoy bind.** If the token paying the module is not the one it is bound to, `status` / `info` carry a `warning`:
  the buy-backs and liquidity go to the bound token's pair, and this token's tax is only distributed by hand.
- **Gas.** A chunk takes about 0.55–0.75M gas, and a split pushes up to 10 payees (each push needs about 365k gas left
  before it). Never set or trim a gas limit: let the wallet estimate it; a lower one reverts `PushStarved`.
- **Where.** BSC testnet set 10 for now; on mainnet the factory is not deployed yet and a combined launch is refused.

```bash
gang combo status --token 0x… [--asset 0x…]   # ratios, payees, buybackLeft / lpLeft / unsplit, owed, next chunk, keeper plan, decoy warning
gang combo distribute --token 0x… [--asset 0x…|wbnb]   # anyone: split the bound quote, or another asset (buy-back + LP share to 0xdead)
gang combo release --token 0x… [--asset 0x…] [--index <i>] [--max <amount>]   # anyone: push a slot what a failed push left owed
gang combo set-payee --token 0x… --to 0x… [--index <i>]   # only that slot's payee (default: your own slot)
gang combo bind --token 0x…                    # anyone: bind (the launch does it)
gang combo chunk --token 0x… [--slippage 1]    # anyone: one chunk now (graduated tokens; one per 4 minutes for both kinds)
```
